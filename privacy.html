What we collect

The app itself does not ask you to create a login or send us personal profile data. Third-party SDKs used for ads and purchases may collect information as described in their policies, including:

Details of what Google collects and how it is used are governed by Google’s Privacy Policy and the applicable AdMob / Play policies.

How we use information

  • Honor Remove Ads after a successful Google Play purchase.

We do not sell your personal information.
